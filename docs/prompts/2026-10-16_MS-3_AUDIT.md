## Decisions Gary needs to make (plain English, read these first)

1. **Is "100% initial compliance" the same as "100% compliant right now"?**
   Froot has no idea of "initial" compliance. There is no onboarding packet and no
   list of "items due at hire". There is one compliance score, and it counts every
   assigned training and every document that applies to the person, whenever it
   was added. If the gate means "100% right now", one new handbook version
   requiring re-signature turns every eligible person red until they sign it.
   *Lean:* use the existing score. The gate passes at 100%, and also passes for
   someone with nothing assigned. Label it "Compliance 100%" (not "initial"). See
   A3 and F2.
2. **Raise eligibility only works where Square labor is switched on.** Hours come
   from Square timecards, on-time comes from Square schedules, and raises come
   from Square wages. All three are only read when the Square labor connection is
   on. Milestones only needs HR. So an org can have Milestones on and no data for
   any raise criterion. *Lean:* with Square labor off, no Raise badge appears
   anywhere, and the settings section says why (F12).
3. **Froot only notices a raise when an ADMIN clicks "Sync roster"** on
   /settings/labor. Nothing syncs wages on a schedule. Until someone clicks, a
   person who just got a raise still looks eligible. *Lean:* add a daily wage sync
   to the existing 11:30 UTC timecard cron. It is one read-only Square call per
   org per day and needs no new permission. Like every cron, it only runs on
   production (F8).
4. **Raise detection cannot be tested on staging with real data.** Staging is
   connected to the real Keva Square account, and Froot never writes to Square.
   The only way to trigger a raise on staging would be to give someone a real
   raise in Square. *Lean:* the fixture proves detection on dev, and staging
   checks the cooldown using the manual "last raise date" (F6).
5. **What happens when a required document doesn't apply to a person?** An ADMIN
   might require a document that is only assigned to one store. Someone at
   another store could then never complete it. *Lean:* "Not assigned to this
   person" counts as not met (yellow), and the reason is shown so the ADMIN can
   fix the assignment (F11).
6. **Size: split into two sessions.** *Lean:* MS-3a builds the settings, the
   criteria and the badge, and touches no wage data. MS-3b builds raise
   detection, the manual last-raise date, the cooldown and the daily wage sync.
   All of the COMP-1 pay risk sits in one reviewable phase. See A10.
7. **The badge disappearing tells a manager that a raise happened.** Ruling 4
   says the badge goes away after a raise. Any manager who can see the badge will
   therefore see it vanish, even one who can't see pay. *Lean:* accept this. The
   amount and the date stay hidden from anyone who can't see that person's pay
   (F7, F10).

Every other fork (F1, F3–F5, F9, F13–F15) has a lean below that I think is

## Phase 1 — Audit

### A1. Wage data

**`SquareTeamMemberWage`** (`prisma/schema.prisma:3161-3255`) has **one row per
member, not one per job**: `@@unique([organizationId, squareTeamMemberId])`
(:3254).
- `jobId` and `jobTitle` come from `wage_setting.job_assignments[0]` only.
  `jobAssignmentCount` records how many jobs the member has, and was measured at
  99/99 members with exactly one job (the schema comment).
- `payType` is Square's raw `HOURLY | SALARY | NONE` string.
- `hourlyRate Decimal(10,2)` and `annualRate Decimal(12,2)` are in **dollars**
  (Square cents ÷ 100). NULL means "not set in Square", never $0.
- Salaried members have `annualRate` plus `squareWeeklyHours`.
- `compConfidential Boolean @default(false)` (:3243) is the COMP-1 flag. It is
  Froot-owned, and the sync never touches it.
- There is no relation to `StaffMember`. The two join on `squareTeamMemberId`.

**`WageSnapshot`** (`schema.prisma:3676-3692`) stores `payType`, `hourlyRate`,
`annualRate`, `jobId`, `wageVersion` and `capturedAt @default(now())`.
- It is append-only, with no unique constraint. The index is
  `(organizationId, squareTeamMemberId, capturedAt)`.
- It has **no confidentiality flag of its own**. The schema comment (:3671-3674)
  requires every reader to apply `SquareTeamMemberWage.compConfidential`.

**How snapshot rows are written.** `syncTeamMemberWages` (`src/lib/labor-roster.ts:138`)
calls `writeRoster`, then `writeWageSnapshots` (:194) inside its own try/catch,
so a failed snapshot step leaves the roster write in place and returns `-1`.
- `wageChanged` (:355-363) compares only `payType`, `hourlyRate` and
  `annualRate`, to the cent.
- The comparison is against the member's **latest snapshot**, read with
  `DISTINCT ON` (:380-385), not against the wage row.
- A member with no snapshot always gets one. That is the baseline.
- `jobId` and `wageVersion` are stored, but a change in them alone does not write
  a row (:351-354).
- The function returns a count (`createMany`), which verify-ms1 C19 pins.

**Every trigger.** `syncTeamMemberWages` has exactly **one** caller:
`POST /api/square/labor/roster/sync` (`route.ts:53`).
- That route needs `requireSquareLabor()` (overlay on), then `square.manage`
  (ADMIN only, not deniable), then a stored Square token.
- Its one UI caller is the "Sync roster" button on /settings/labor
  (`labor-settings-client.tsx:1540`).
- **There is no cron.** `vercel.json` registers sales-reconcile, pace-alerts,
  checklist-day-close, labor-timecards, labor-scheduled-shifts, engagement-prune
  and calendar-materialize, and none of them imports labor-roster.
- Staff import and resync (`api/staff/sync-square`) write `StaffMember` and store
  assignments only. They write no wages and no snapshots.
- The route header (`sync/route.ts:15-17`) says freshness also rests on "the
  debounced refresh the Positions card schedules". **No such debounce exists**
  (agent sweep of `labor-settings-client.tsx`; the card's `useEffect` only does a
  GET). See triage #2.
- The roster sync defaults to `status: "ACTIVE"`, so INACTIVE members are not
  refreshed.

**Square cost per run.** One `POST /v2/team-members/search` (limit 200; about 99
members, so one page). It also calls `GET /v2/labor/team-member-wages`, but only
when a member lacks `wage_setting`, which was measured at zero. Scope:
`EMPLOYEES_READ`, which is already held.

**How stale can a wage be?** There is no limit. Froot sees a raise only when an
ADMIN clicks "Sync roster". A wage changed twice between two syncs shows only the
final value. `detectedAt` is the sync time, not the date Square changed the wage.
`SquareTimecard.wageHourlyRate` is refreshed daily by the timecard cron, but that
is the historical rate per shift and never feeds `WageSnapshot`.

### A3. Compliance — "outstanding" vs "initial"

There is **one engine**: `computeStaffComplianceDetails()` in
`src/lib/hr-compliance.ts:258-638`. It reads live records and stores no snapshots.
Every compliance surface is a view of its item list:
- the % column on /staff (`getStaffComplianceSummaries`, :644)
- the Compliance tab (`getStaffComplianceDetail`, :664)
- /hr/compliance (`getOrgComplianceRollup`, :741)
- "outstanding"/"owed" (`openComplianceItems`, :691; `summarizeOwed`, :722)
- the By Document pivot

**Required items.**
- Documents: `kind: "Acknowledgment", isActive, requiresAcknowledgment`
  (`complianceDocumentWhere`, :957-964) that reach the member through their
  audience (`grantedToStaff`) and have a current version.
- Trainings: **every** `TrainingAssignment` row for the member.

**100%** means `completedCount / requiredTotal` with `Math.round` (:185, :615-631).
- `requiredTotal = 0` gives `pct: null`.
- "Fully compliant" in the rollups is `requiredTotal > 0 && pct === 100` (:820).

**"Initial" does not exist in code.**
- There is no onboarding, packet or "at hire" concept; the only hit is the
  "Coming soon" marketing copy in `milestones-client.tsx:222`.
- Nothing expires or recurs: one assignment per module (`@@unique`) and no due
  dates on documents.
- An obligation can come back three ways:
  - a **rehire** (`signingCycle` +1) makes earlier document signatures "needs
    re-sign";
  - a **new document version** with `requiresReacknowledgment`;
  - **adding a lesson** to a completed module, which drops it back to InProgress
    (`training.ts:137-163`).

**So "any outstanding compliance = red" and "100% compliance" describe the same
check today.** "Outstanding" means at least one item not complete, which is
exactly pct < 100. The only difference is a member with zero items, whose pct is
null and who has nothing outstanding. "Initial" adds nothing the code can tell
apart. See F2.

### A4. Hours

**Source.** `SquareTimecard` (`schema.prisma:3028-3104`) stores `startAt`,
`endAt?` (null while open), `status`, `breakPaidMinutes`, `breakUnpaidMinutes` and
`storeId` (resolved from the Square location).
- It joins to `StaffMember` on `squareTeamMemberId`, which is unique per org.
- All times are UTC; the reader converts to the store's zone.

**Sync.** `syncTimecardsForStore` (`labor-actuals.ts:660`) has three callers:
- the cron `/api/cron/labor-timecards` (`30 11 * * *`), which re-reads **3
  store-local days** per store (`RECONCILE_DAYS = 3`, route.ts:47) and is
  overlay-gated;
- a dashboard load, which syncs today only;
- a manual ADMIN sync for any range.

Two consequences:
- **Production history is complete from the day the cron went live.** A manager
  correction made more than 3 days later is missed (route.ts:41-43).
- **Staging has no cron**, so its timecards come only from dashboard loads, one
  day at a time.

**Paid time.** `paidMinutesOf` (`labor-actuals.ts:236-243`) is gross time minus
`breakUnpaidMinutes`; paid breaks count. This is the house definition, already
used by actuals, the schedule and the inspector. Overtime is not modelled:
`otApplied` is always false (:80-85) by ruling, and doesn't matter for counting
hours.

**Labor week.** `src/lib/labor-week.ts` is the only week helper.
- Weeks are hard-coded to **start Monday**, computed on `yyyy-mm-dd` strings with
  no zone (`mondayOfWeekStr`, :7).
- Square's workweek settings are **not read anywhere**. `TIMECARDS_SETTINGS_READ`
  is held but unused, and there is no org setting for the week's start day.
- Callers pass a store-local date in.

**There is no weekly-hours helper per member.**
- The inspector sums paid minutes per member for a **single day**
  (`labor-inspector.ts:447-515`).
- `getLaborActuals` aggregates per store with no member id.
- `weeklyHours`/`weeklyHoursOverride` on the roster are contracted hours, not
  worked hours.
- **MS-3 needs a new helper** (see the plan).

### A5. Trainings and the Document Library

**Trainings.**
- `TrainingModule` (`schema.prisma:2437`): `isActive`, `isArchived`, `appliesTo`
  (`all`/`selected`), no versioning.
- `TrainingAssignment` (:2576): `@@unique([trainingModuleId, staffMemberId])`,
  `status` NotStarted | InProgress | Completed, `dueDate?`, `certifiedAt?`.
- **Completed** means `status === "Completed"`: every lesson has a progress row
  and, if the module has a quiz, a Passed attempt (`training.ts:134-163`).
- Certification is shown but does not gate completion. The compliance engine
  uses the same test (`hr-compliance.ts:597`).
- A module only becomes required for someone when an assignment row exists.
  Its `appliesTo` only controls who it *can* be assigned to.

**Documents.**
- `HrDocument` (:1886): `kind`, `appliesTo` (default `"selected"`),
  `requiresAcknowledgment`, `isActive` (archive sets it false).
- Audience: `HrDocumentGrant` rows (STORE or STAFF), or `appliesTo: "all"`.
- Signing: `HrDocumentVersion` (`isCurrent`, `requiresReacknowledgment`),
  checkpoints, acknowledgments, `HrSignedRecord`.
- **Completed** means an `HrSignedRecord` for the member's **current signing
  cycle**. A record on an earlier version counts unless the current version has
  `requiresReacknowledgment` (`documentCompletion`,
  `src/lib/hr-completion.ts:222-281`).
- Acknowledged checkpoints alone show "in progress", never complete.
- Only `Acknowledgment` documents with `requiresAcknowledgment` create an
  obligation. Reference, Link and FillableForm documents never do.

**Listing for checkboxes.**
- Trainings: `GET /api/hr/training` (ADMIN, every module); the server-side query
  in `staff/[id]/page.tsx:512-523` (active, not archived, `{id, title}`).
- **Checkbox pattern to copy:** the "Assign training" dialog,
  `staff/[id]/staff-training.tsx:449-480` (plain `<input type="checkbox">` list).
- Documents: **there is no list API**. The library is loaded server-side in
  `hr/documents/page.tsx:41-61`. MS-3 loads the eligible documents
  (`complianceDocumentWhere`) in `/staff/milestones/page.tsx` and passes
  `{id, title}` as props, so it needs no new read route.
- Both trainings and documents are org-wide. Each has an optional category.

### A6. MS-2 on-time function

The function is `getOnTimeForStaff(org, members: AttendanceMember[], settings,
now)` at `src/lib/attendance.ts:243`.
- `AttendanceMember` is `{ id, squareTeamMemberId, assignedStoreIds, range: {
  from, to } }`, where `range` is an **inclusive pair of store-local dates,
  per member**.
- It is batched: one read each for shifts, closed cards, open cards and sync
  state.
- It returns `Map<id, OnTimeResult>`, where `OnTimeResult` is `{ status, reason,
  onTime, total, percent (exact), displayPercent (floored), range }`.
- `status` is `green | amber | red | not-enough | no-schedule | not-linked`.
- `reason` is `square-labor-off | no-published-shifts | null`.

The grey states are what MS-3 needs to map:
- **Overlay off:** no rows are read; the result is `no-schedule` with reason
  `square-labor-off`, or `not-linked` (:262-267).
- **No published shift** at any of the member's stores in range: `no-schedule`
  with `no-published-shifts` (:194-199).
- **`total < onTimeMinShifts`:** `not-enough`, with `percent` still filled when
  total > 0 (:205-207).

MS-3 passes its own `range` and ignores MS-2's red/amber/green; it compares the
exact `percent` to `raiseMinOnTimePct`. Grace and the minimum shift count come
from the `settings` argument, so the org's grace applies. `MilestoneSettings`
(`schema.prisma:3710-3733`) has one row per org, and its comment already names
MS-3 as the next tenant.

## Phase 2 — Plan

### Schema (additive only)

```prisma
model MilestoneSettings {
  // … MS-2 fields unchanged …
  /// MS-3 ruling 5: ADMIN toggle. Applies to raise eligibility only; tenure
  /// always counts from the start date.
  raiseComplianceGate    Boolean @default(true)
  raiseMinHoursPerWeek   Int     @default(20)   // whole hours, 1–60
  raiseConsecutiveWeeks  Int     @default(8)    // completed labor weeks, 1–26
  raiseMinOnTimePct      Int     @default(90)   // 1–100, exact % compared
  raiseCooldownDays      Int     @default(90)   // 30/60/90/180 or custom 1–730
  raiseRequiredTrainings MilestoneRaiseTraining[]
  raiseRequiredDocuments MilestoneRaiseDocument[]
}

model MilestoneRaiseTraining {          // join table: required trainings
  milestoneSettingsId String
  trainingModuleId    String
  createdAt DateTime @default(now())
  settings MilestoneSettings @relation(fields: [milestoneSettingsId], references: [id], onDelete: Cascade)
  module   TrainingModule    @relation(fields: [trainingModuleId], references: [id], onDelete: Cascade)
  @@id([milestoneSettingsId, trainingModuleId])
}

model MilestoneRaiseDocument {          // join table: required documents
  milestoneSettingsId String
  hrDocumentId        String
  createdAt DateTime @default(now())
  settings MilestoneSettings @relation(fields: [milestoneSettingsId], references: [id], onDelete: Cascade)
  document HrDocument        @relation(fields: [hrDocumentId], references: [id], onDelete: Cascade)
  @@id([milestoneSettingsId, hrDocumentId])
}

// MS-3b only:
model StaffMember {
  // …
  /// F6: ADMIN-entered date of the last raise BEFORE Froot's wage history
  /// (or one Froot missed). Store-local civil date. Pay data: shown only to
  /// viewers who can see this member's pay.
  lastRaiseDate        DateTime? @db.Date
  lastRaiseSetByUserId String?
  lastRaiseSetAt       DateTime?
}
```

Notes on the schema:
- **Two join tables, not one polymorphic one**, so each has a real foreign key and
  cascades when a module or document is deleted.
- Archived trainings and inactive documents are **skipped** at evaluation time
  and flagged "archived" in settings. A hard delete cascades.
- The default values are a lean. Gary can rule different numbers.
- `verify-ms2`'s "defaults equal the schema @defaults" check is extended to the
  new fields.

**Raise record vs derive on read. Lean: DERIVE on read** from `WageSnapshot`
plus `lastRaiseDate`:
- `WageSnapshot` is already append-only and written only on change, so it is a
  ready-made change log.
- A raise table would be a second copy of the same fact, with its own writer,
  backfill and drift.
- A member's history is a few rows, and one query covers a whole roster.
- MS-4's "notify exactly once" needs its own sent-log anyway (MS-1's note), so a
  raise table would not save it anything.

### The one eligibility function

```ts

RaiseEligibility = {
  status: "green" | "yellow" | "red" | "cooldown" | "off"
  criteria: Array<{ key: "compliance"|"hours"|"onTime"|"trainings"|"documents"|"cooldown",
                    state: "met" | "not-met" | "blocked" | "no-data" | "disabled",
                    detail: string /* never an amount or a date unless the
                                      viewer may see pay — see redaction */ }>
  cooldownUntil: string | null      // yyyy-mm-dd, redacted per F10
  lastRaise: { on: string; source: "square" | "manual"; fromRate?: string; toRate?: string } | null  // redacted per F10
}
```

- It is **batched**, the same shape as `getOnTimeForStaff`, so the /staff list
  makes one call.
- The single-member signature is a wrapper. MS-4 and MS-5 call the same
  function.
- A separate pure `redactForViewer(result, payVisible: boolean)` returns the only
  shape that ever reaches a page or a response. When `payVisible` is false it
  **deletes** `lastRaise` and `cooldownUntil` rather than nulling them, and puts a
  dateless sentence in the cooldown `detail`.
- `payVisible` per member is `canSeeWages(org, actor) && compVisibleOnRow(actor,
  wageRow)`, which fails closed with no wage row (the `compVisibleForMember`
  rule).

**Per-criterion evaluation:**
- **Compliance** (when the gate is on): the existing `pct`.
  - `100`, or `null` with no items: met.
  - Anything else: blocked (red).
- **Hours.** New helper `weeklyPaidHours(members, weeks)`:
  - one `SquareTimecard` read for the whole window, summed with `paidMinutesOf`;
  - each card is dated by its `startAt` in **its own store's** zone, then keyed
    to `mondayOfWeekStr`;
  - closed cards only.
  - The window is the last Y **completed** Monday-to-Sunday weeks before the
    current week in the member's display zone (F3).
- **On-time:** `getOnTimeForStaff` with `range = {Monday of first week, Sunday of
  last week}`.
- **Trainings:** each required, non-archived module must have a
  `TrainingAssignment.status === "Completed"` for the member.
- **Documents:** `documentCompletion(...) === "signed"` for each required active
  document. This reuses the compliance engine's per-document item where the
  document is in the member's audience, and gives "not assigned" otherwise (F11).
- **Cooldown** (3b): the latest of the last detected raise and `lastRaiseDate`,
  plus `raiseCooldownDays`, as a store-local date.

### Forks for Gary — each with Claude's lean

**F1. Colours.** *Lean as the prompt states, made exact:*
- **RED**, a hard blocker: the compliance gate is on and fails, OR on-time has
  data (a `percent` with at least the minimum shifts) and is below the minimum.
- **YELLOW:** no hard blocker, but at least one enabled criterion is not met or

- The tab lists each criterion as met / not met / no data.
- A terminated member gets no badge at all.

**F2. Compliance gate meaning (A3).** There are not two checks in code; there is
one. *Lean:* the gate passes when the existing compliance score is 100%, or the
member has no compliance items. A new required document or a rehire turns a
member red until they finish it, which is the score's existing behaviour. The
label reads "Compliance 100%". **Gary decides** whether "initial" was meant to
narrow this, for example to items assigned within N days of start. That narrower
version has no data behind it today: document audiences carry no "from" date.

**F3. Hours.** *Lean:*
- Paid hours (gross minus unpaid breaks, the house rule) in each of the last Y
  **completed** Monday-to-Sunday weeks.
- Each week must be ≥ X hours. All stores combined. The current partial week is
  ignored.
