## Why

Froot syncs Square **items and their variations** (sizes) as SalesItems and
attaches recipes to them. It does **not** sync **modifiers** — "Add Peanut
Butter", "Sub Almond Milk", "Add Red Bull", "No Banana", toppers, boosts. So:

- **Inventory:** every add-on's ingredients go uncounted in usage.
- **Nutrition:** add-ons carry no nutrition; customised drinks can't be shown.
- **BevSpot:** the Las Brisas sales-items export
  (`/Users/garythomas/Downloads/Keva_Las_Brisas_#14_sales_items_2026-10-31.xlsx`)
  has **~491 rows skipped by BEV-1 as "not one of your Square items"** — mostly
  modifier recipes keyed by Square **modifier** ids (e.g. "Add Peanut Butter
  (1 scoop Blended in)" → Peanut Butter 1 oz (dry); "Sub Non-Dairy Oat Milk
  Yogurt" → Vanilla Yogurt −10 fl oz, Oatmilk Vanilla +10 fl oz; "No Banana"
  → Bananas −1.6 oz (dry)). BEV-1 F7 also skipped every negative quantity.

N-5 brings modifiers in as first-class, read-only Square objects that can carry
a recipe, and imports BevSpot's modifier recipes onto them.

**Standing rule:** nothing is promoted to production until Gary confirms it on
staging. Remember staging's Square catalog differs from production's
(different ids), so the BevSpot modifier import can only be proven on
production with a preview first — same as BEV-1.

## Proposed scope (the audit confirms or splits it)

1. **Sync** Square modifier lists and modifiers (catalog `MODIFIER_LIST`),
   and which items use which lists (`item_data.modifier_list_info`), into new
   read-only tables, on the same ADMIN "Sync from Square" path as items.
   Deleted/archived modifiers handled like deleted SalesItems.
2. **Modifier recipes:** a modifier can have a recipe, built and edited like a
   menu-item recipe, with one rule change — **lines may be negative** for
   modifier recipes only (removals and substitutions). Menu-item and prep
   recipes keep refusing negatives.
3. **Cost & nutrition of a modifier** = its recipe's lines (can net negative).
   Shown in the recipe editor and on a **Modifiers** view in Menu Nutrition
   ("Add Peanut Butter: +170 kcal, +$0.18"). Not on the public menu in N-5.
4. **BevSpot modifier import:** extend the BEV-1 importer so a row whose
   Square id matches a synced **modifier** imports as that modifier's recipe —
   BEV-1 rules otherwise (exact ingredient match, never overwrite, preview
   first, prep rows as sub-recipes), with negatives allowed for these rows.
5. **Sales usage (audit decides if it fits here or becomes N-5b):** if Froot's
   sales-driven usage reads Square order line items, include each line's
   applied modifiers × their recipes in theoretical usage.

Out of scope: size-scaled modifiers (one recipe per modifier, all sizes),
public-menu add-ons, customised-drink labels, N-4, N-6.
