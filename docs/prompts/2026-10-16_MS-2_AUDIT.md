   clock-in to a scheduled start. So F2's premise, "whatever the inspector

   someone loads the dashboard (2 stores per load, 30-minute cooldown, window

## Phase 1 — Audit

### A1. The inspector's on-time logic

**Where:** `src/lib/labor-inspector.ts` (pure `assembleDayInspector` plus the DB
read `getDayInspector`), served by `src/app/api/labor/day-inspector/route.ts`
and rendered by `src/app/(app)/labor/inspector/day-inspector-client.tsx`.
Fixture: `scripts/verify-labor-inspector.ts`.

**It has no "late" or "on-time" concept.** There are six flags: `OPEN-STALE`,
`OPEN-LONG`, `DOUBLE`, `UNMAPPED`, `NO-SHOW` and `UNSCHEDULED`. None of them
compares `SquareTimecard.startAt` to `effectiveStartAt`.

What it does define, which MS-2 should reuse rather than restate:

- **Pairing (timecard ↔ scheduled shift).** The same Square team member, at the
  same store (both reads are per store), whose half-open intervals overlap by at
  least `SHOWED_UP_MIN_OVERLAP_MINUTES = 15` (`labor-inspector.ts:88`). An open
  card's end is `clockedEndMs(card, ceiling)`, where ceiling = min(now, end of
  day).
- **NO-SHOW.** A live, assigned shift that no card of that member at that store
  overlaps by 15 minutes or more. It is only evaluated once
  `noShowEligible(start, syncedThroughMs, NO_SHOW_GRACE_MINUTES = 20)` holds,
  meaning start + 20 minutes is at or before min(now, lastTimecardSyncOkAt)
  (`labor-inspector.ts:98`, `:141`). A shift that is not yet eligible is
  "pending" and counted nowhere.
- **UNSCHEDULED.** A card with no shift of that member overlapping it by 15
  minutes or more. It is evidence-positive, so it is not horizon-gated.
- **Suppression.** `scheduleComparable()` turns both NO-SHOW and UNSCHEDULED off
  when schedule health is `never` or `synced-empty`, or there are zero shifts
  that day (`:163`).

**Grace for lateness: none.** `NO_SHOW_GRACE_MINUTES = 20` is **not** a lateness
grace. It is how long to wait before an absence means anything. Reading it as
"20 minutes late is still on time" would be a misreading, and the plan
deliberately does not do that (F2).

**Early clock-in:** irrelevant to the inspector. Any 15-minute overlap pairs.

### A4. Edge cases — how the inspector treats each

| Case | Inspector today | MS-2 lean |
|---|---|---|
| Scheduled, no timecard (no-show) | NO-SHOW, once start + 20 minutes is within the synced horizon | Counts as **not on time**, only once eligible. Shifts not yet eligible are excluded from both numerator and denominator (F3) |
| Timecard, no shift (unscheduled) | UNSCHEDULED | **Excluded entirely** (F3) |
| Several shifts in one day | Each shift is paired independently. Any card of that member overlapping it by 15 minutes or more matches | Same. For lateness, use the **earliest** card that pairs with the shift. A single 9–5 card covering 9–1 and 1–5 shifts makes both on time |
| Shift across midnight | Instants are compared, so pairing is unaffected. The timeline shows the shift on both days | Pairing by instants is unchanged. **For the window, a shift belongs to the store-local date of its start, in its own store's zone** |
| More than one store | Reads are per store, so pairing never crosses stores. Scheduled at A and clocked in at B shows NO-SHOW at A and UNSCHEDULED at B | Same-store pairing, the one definition. The % spans **all** of a member's stores (F10) |
| Draft vs published | Ghosts are drawn from `effective*` (published, else draft) and labelled "draft" | **Count published shifts only** (F9) |
| Unassigned (open) shifts | Never a no-show, and counted separately | Irrelevant. There is no member to attribute them to |
| Tombstoned shifts | Filtered | Filtered |
| Still-open card | Overlap is measured to the ceiling | Same. Lateness only needs the card's `startAt` |

### A7. Settings storage — the house pattern

Two patterns exist:

- **A plain `Organization` column** for a single scalar or switch:
  `calendarEnabled`, `milestonesEnabled`, `paceAlertsEnabled`,

### Forks for Gary — each with Claude's lean

**F0. Design bundle.** F0 (2026-10-15, Gary's words): *"The design bundle comes

**F1. Look-back window.** *Lean:* rolling, ADMIN-set, default 30 days. A shift
belongs to the store-local date of its start, **in its own store's zone**, so a
Denver shift is dated in Denver even for a member whose primary store is in
Pacific. The window is the N store-local days ending today. Today's shifts
count once they become eligible (F3). Bounds: 7–90 days.

**F2. Grace — the inspector has none, so this is a new definition.** *Lean:*
on time = the earliest paired clock-in is at or before the scheduled start plus
`onTimeGraceMinutes`, **compared to the minute** (9:00:59 against a 9:00 start
at grace 0 is on time; 9:01:00 is late). This adds one ADMIN setting, default 0,
bounds 0–30. Clocking in early is always on time. **The inspector does not gain
a LATE flag in MS-2.** It has no lateness concept to disagree with, and adding
one changes inspector behaviour, which is your ruling. If you want it there
too, it is a small follow-up that reads the same setting. The inspector's
20-minute NO-SHOW wait is a different concept and is reused only for no-show
eligibility (F3).

**F3. No-shows and unscheduled.** *Lean:* as the prompt says. A no-show counts
as not on time. Unscheduled cards are left out. **Added:** a no-show counts only
once the inspector's `noShowEligible` holds (start + 20 minutes within the
timecard sync's horizon). A shift whose timecards Froot has not heard about yet
is excluded rather than counted late. This is the inspector's A12 rule, reused.

**F4. Too few shifts.** *Lean:* fewer than `onTimeMinShifts` eligible shifts in
the window shows a grey "Not enough shifts" badge. Default 5, bounds 1–50.

**F5. No schedule data, and the overlay gate.** *Lean:* a grey "No schedule
data" badge when none of the member's stores has published shift rows in the
window. **Added:** when the org's Square labor overlay is off (any of Labor
module, `squareLaborAvailable` or `squareLaborEnabled` false), the badge is the
same grey and **no rows are read.** This is the inspector's "the honest answer
is the surface doesn't exist" rule: stale rows from before the toggle was
turned off are not drawn. The Attendance setup section says it needs Square
labor and Square scheduled shifts, and stays editable so an admin can set
thresholds in advance.

**F6. Who sees the badge.** *Lean:* the Milestones tab's gate. Milestones on
(HR available + HR on + toggle) and role ADMIN or MANAGER. MANAGER scope is
already enforced by the roster query and the profile's 404. STORE and STAFF
never see it, and they cannot reach /staff anyway.

**F7. Compact badge on /staff.** *Lean:* **yes.** The data is in the DB (A2),
loaded in one batched read for the whole roster. It sits beside the name like
the `You` and `Terminated` badges, so no new column. It shows the percent and
colour only, with the grey states as a short word and a tooltip.

**F8. Threshold edges.** *Lean:* as the prompt says. Red is `< red`, green is
`>= green`, and amber is in between. The setup page and the route both refuse
`red >= green`. Percentages are whole numbers 1–100. The displayed percent is
rounded **after** the colour decision, so 89.6% shows "90%" but stays amber.
*Alternative:* decide on the rounded value. Your call; the lean avoids a badge
that turns green only because of display rounding.

**F9. Draft-only shifts (new).** About 63 of 462 shifts were draft-only at S1b.
Staff were never told about a draft shift. *Lean:* **count published shifts
only** (`effectiveSource = "published"`). Missing an unpublished draft is not a
no-show.

**F10. Several stores, and MANAGER scope (new).** *Lean:* pairing stays
same-store (the inspector's rule, the one definition). Clocking in at the wrong
store is a no-show at the scheduled one. The percentage covers **all** of the
member's stores, including ones outside a MANAGER's scope. It is the employee's
attendance, the same whole-employee view the tab already gives, and it exposes
no store-level detail.

**F11. Unlinked and terminated members (new).** *Lean:* a member with no
`squareTeamMemberId` gets a grey "Not linked to Square" badge. A terminated
member's window ends at their store-local termination date (MS-1 F4's
precedent for tenure).

**F12. Inspector extraction (new, small).** *Lean:* export one pairing predicate
