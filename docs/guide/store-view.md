This is the shift screen. Open the store, work down the list, submit at the end.

If you are assigned to exactly one store you land straight on it. If you have
more than one, pick the store first.

## Working the list

Tasks are grouped into sections in the order they were built. Tick each one as
you do it.

**Critical tasks are outlined in red.** They are the ones that decide whether the
checklist counts as compliant, which is worth knowing before you reach the end
rather than after.

**Some tasks require a photo.** Those show a Take Photo button and will not tick
without one.

## Assigning a task to someone

A task can be assigned to a staff member at the store. The list of names comes
from the Staff Directory, so anyone missing from that list cannot be picked here.

## Handoff notes

Above the sections you will see what the previous shift left for this checklist,
and you can leave a note for the next one. The note travels with the checklist,
not with the person, so it is there for whoever opens it next.

## Submitting

Submit closes the checklist for the day.

**If any critical task is unticked when you submit, the checklist is recorded as
Non-Compliant rather than Completed.** It still submits. The status is the
record of what was outstanding at the moment it went in, and it does not change
afterwards.

Non-critical tasks left unticked do not change the status.

## If the day closes first

A checklist nobody submits is swept to **Missed** by the overnight close. Missed
is a closed fact — it cannot be submitted late, and it appears the morning after
rather than at the moment the day ends.
