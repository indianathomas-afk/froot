Daily Checklists is the review side. Running a checklist happens in the store
view; this page is where you look at what came back.

The store filter at the top narrows the list. You only ever see stores you have
access to, so the filter shortens a list that is already yours.

## The five statuses

- **Not Started** — the checklist exists for the day and nobody has opened it.
- **In Progress** — someone has started ticking and has not submitted.
- **Completed** — submitted with every critical task done.
- **Non-Compliant** — submitted with at least one critical task outstanding.
- **Missed** — the day closed without a submission.

**Non-Compliant and Missed are different failures.** Non-Compliant means someone
was there and submitted anyway. Missed means nothing was submitted at all.

## Late and overdue

A checklist can be Completed and still be flagged as completed late — submitted
after its window closed. The status says what happened; the late flag says when.

Overdue marks a checklist still open past its window and not yet swept.

## Why a status does not change afterwards

A submitted status is a record of the moment it was submitted. Ticking something
afterwards does not move a Non-Compliant checklist to Completed, and a Missed one
cannot be reopened.
