The dashboard is where the app opens and where most other screens send you back
to. What it shows depends on your access and on which modules your organisation
has, so two people can open it and see different cards.

The store selector is shared with Messages — change it in one and the other
follows.

## Today's checklist

The shift card links straight into today's checklist for the selected store. On
a shift this is usually the only thing on the page you need.

## Labor coverage

Where the Labor module is on, the coverage card shows how the day is staffed
against the plan.

**The "on floor" popover lists people by name with their clock-in times.** It is
closed until you open it. Worth knowing before you show the dashboard on a
screen anyone else can see.

## Corporate updates

The most recent update from head office, with who posted it. Where nothing has
been posted, the card does not appear.

## Counts

Where Inventory is on and your access allows it, the dashboard carries a days
since last count figure. It is a prompt to go and count, not a stock number.
