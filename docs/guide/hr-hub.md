This is the front door to everything HR. It is a set of cards, not a screen you
work in — each one takes you somewhere that does the actual job.

## The cards, and why you may see fewer than someone else

The cards are filtered by your role, so two people opening this page can see
different things. That is deliberate, not a fault:

- **Staff Directory** — the people who work for you.
- **Document Library** — handbooks, policies and forms to read or sign.
- **Compliance** — who has finished what, and who has not.
- **Agreement Forms** — one-off agreements like a key issue or an equipment
  loan.
- **Training** — the modules people work through.
- **Signed Records** — the completed, executed copies.

If a card is missing, it is not hidden from you as a punishment and there is
nothing to switch on. Your role does not include that part of HR.

## Where to start

If you are setting HR up for the first time, the order that causes least rework
is: get the Staff Directory right, add the documents people need to sign, then
build training on top. Compliance and Signed Records fill themselves in as
people work through the first two — they are readouts, not things you populate.
